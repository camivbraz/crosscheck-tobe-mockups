Real-time Cross-Check at TO Packing Scan

PDA > Packing > Scan package. Cross-Check now runs when the package is scanned, while it is still in the operator's hands; the existing check at TO closure stays. Open any state to run the full flow — every page is interactive (scan field, pop-up, re-scan, dismiss, supervisor PIN).

1 · Scan · Scanning — correct destinationPackage belongs to the TO destination: accepted, operator keeps scanning. No change from today.
2 · Cross-Check pop-up · Wrong destination pop-upRaised at scan time. Shows package vs TO destination. Primary: Re-scan Package. Discreet close (×) top-right.
2 · Cross-Check pop-up · Re-scan · different packageIf a different package is scanned, the pop-up stays and asks for the flagged package.
3 · Re-scan · Correction confirmedPackage did not enter the TO and goes back to the conveyor. No pendency registered.
4 · Dismiss · Pendency registeredClosing the pop-up registers a pendency on the TO with package ID, operator ID and timestamp.
4 · Dismiss · Pendency listTap the Cross-Check row in the TO context to review registered pendencies.
5 · Close TO · Pendencies · supervisor PINComplete lists all pendencies and requests the supervisor PIN to release them.
5 · Close TO · Incorrect PINWrong PIN is rejected inline; the TO is not packed.
5 · Close TO · No supervisor · stays PackingKeep Packing closes the sheet; the TO remains in Packing status.
6 · Packed · Packed · no pendencyWithout pendencies the TO goes straight to Packed.
